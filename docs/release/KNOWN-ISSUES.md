## Severity scale (A4 triage, 2026-10-02)
| Severity | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Critical** | Data of one school exposed to another, records lost or corrupted at scale, or a core pilot workflow unusable with no workaround |
| **High** | A security weakness or wrong data in a core workflow; any workaround is manual or risky |
| **Med** | Degrades a workflow, its diagnosability or its data quality; a safe workaround exists |
| **Low** | Cosmetic, configuration/documentation, or outside the pilot scope |

Severity is recorded **as found**; a closed item keeps its severity. Pilot exit (A4) needs **no open Critical or High** here and in KNOWN-GAPS — the Product Owner accepts the triage by signing A4.
